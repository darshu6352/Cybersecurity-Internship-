Finding: JWT token stored in Local Storage.

Security Risks:
- XSS attack ke through token access ho sakta hai.
- Stolen token ka misuse ho sakta hai.
Recommendations:
1. Sensitive authentication tokens ke liye HttpOnly cookies consider karo.
2. Cookies par Secure aur appropriate SameSite flags configure karo.
3. Token ki expiry short rakho.
4. Logout ke time session/token invalidate karo.
5. XSS protection implement karo.
